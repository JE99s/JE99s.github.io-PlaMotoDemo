About Platinum Motorsports Collision Center

A dedicated team of automotive restoration experts, proudly serving our local community with honest work and transparent pricing.

Our Story

Built On Craftsmanship, Not Shortcuts

Platinum Motorsports Collision Center was founded on a simple idea: every vehicle that comes through our doors should leave looking and driving like it never left the showroom. Our technicians treat every repair — from a minor bumper scuff to a full structural rebuild — with the same attention to detail and pride in the finished result.

We handle the insurance paperwork, the parts sourcing, and the color-matching so you don't have to. All you have to do is drop off your vehicle and let us take it from there.
